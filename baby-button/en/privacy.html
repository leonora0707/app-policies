Privacy Policy

Hiroki Satomura (hereinafter referred to as the "Developer") establishes the following privacy policy (hereinafter referred to as the "Policy") regarding the handling of user information in the smartphone application "Baby Button" (hereinafter referred to as the "App") provided by the Developer.

1. Collection of Personal Information and Use of Device Features

The App is designed with children's safety as the top priority and does not collect personally identifiable information such as the user's name, address, phone number, or email address.
In addition, to provide the customizable features of the App, we request access permissions for the following device features:

  • Microphone: Used solely to record custom audio for the buttons.
  • Photos (Camera Roll): Used solely to set custom images for the buttons.

All audio and image data obtained through these permissions are saved locally on the user's device only. They are never transmitted, saved, or collected on the Developer's or any third party's external servers.
